Important Guidelines
  • Attendance can only be marked for the past 7 days
  • Weekends (Saturday & Sunday) are automatically marked
  • National holidays cannot be marked for attendance
  • Future dates are locked and cannot be selected
  • Select multiple dates to mark attendance in bulk
Pro Tips
  • Mark attendance daily for accurate records
  • Use "Outdoor Duty" for field work assignments
  • Check your attendance history regularly
  • Contact HR for attendance corrections beyond 7 days
  • Maintain consistency for performance evaluations

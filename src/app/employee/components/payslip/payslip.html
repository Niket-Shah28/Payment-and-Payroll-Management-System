Understanding Your Payslip
  • Basic Salary: Your fixed monthly salary component
  • HRA: House Rent Allowance for accommodation expenses
  • DA: Dearness Allowance to offset inflation
  • PF: Provident Fund contribution for retirement savings
  • Net Salary: Final amount after all deductions

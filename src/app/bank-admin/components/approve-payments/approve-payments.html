How Payment Approval Works

As a bank admin, you review payment requests submitted by organizations and vendors. Approve requests to process payments or reject to return them for revision.

Security Measures

All payment transactions are encrypted and logged. Only approved payments are processed through the banking system with complete audit trails.

Processing Timeline

Approved payments are processed within 24 hours. Monthly salary disbursements are prioritized during the salary period.
